1 · Summary

The gap we can fill

Uzbek Muslims already have strong, trusted question-and-answer sources. The biggest is savollar.islom.uz ("Zikr ahlidan so'rang"), with 75,000+ answers on the site and a Telegram channel of about 150,000. The Muslim Board's muslim.uz has its own Fatvo hay'ati answers and a call center on Telegram and WhatsApp. So the need is not "a first Uzbek Q&A site".

What none of them offer, as far as we could verify, is the combination of:

  • Uzbek and English side by side, for diaspora, students, converts and the next generation.
  • Answers with visible evidence (Qur'an, hadith with grading, madhhab views) and a named scholar plus a second reviewer, as SeekersGuidance does.
  • Search that understands Uzbek: Latin and Cyrillic, and every spelling of o‘ and g‘.
  • A tracked question with a number and a status, so askers are not left waiting in a Telegram inbox.
Positioning I recommend: a Hanafi–Maturidi platform that works alongside the Muslim Board and existing scholars, not against them. Uzbekistan regulates religious content closely (in May 2026 the state launched a bot that checks whether religious material is approved), and the Board publicly warned people against taking religious answers from ChatGPT. Credibility and alignment will matter more than features. See D1 and D2.

4 · Synthesis

What to borrow, what to avoid

From SeekersGuidance, AskImam

Two scholars per answer

One writes, a senior one approves. Show both names. This is our main trust signal.

From FatwaQA, Dar al-Ifta

A question number

Every asker gets a ticket like AQ-24817 and can see its status. No more "did anyone read my message?"

From IslamQA.org

Browse by scholar and madhhab

Scholar profiles with credentials, and filters that say which school an answer follows.

From islom.uz Telegram

Meet people on Telegram

Accept questions and send answers through a Telegram bot, with the website as the permanent, searchable home.

From Muslimaat.uz, AboutIslam

Separate desks

A women's desk answered by female scholars, plus family counseling and a desk for new Muslims.

From umma.ru Islamic AI

AI only as a librarian

If we use AI at all, it finds and cites already-published answers. It never writes a ruling.

Avoid (Darulifta Deoband)

Regulatory blind spots

A strong site was blocked by a government. We should know Uzbekistan's rules before launch.

Avoid (Ask Mufti Online)

Paywalls on religion

Paid access to scholars erodes trust. Fund through waqf-style donations instead.

5 · Proposal

Feature set

MVP = needed for launch. Phase 2 = after launch, once we have scholars and traffic.

F1

Search-first archiveMVP

The home page is a search box. Search treats Latin and Cyrillic as the same (namoz = намоз) and all spellings of o‘/g‘ (o', oʻ, o`, o’) as equal. English and Uzbek results appear together, labeled by language.

F2

Guided "Ask a question"MVP

A short form: topic, question, language, and whether it may be published. As the person types, matching answered questions appear, so many people find their answer without submitting.

F3

Privacy choicesMVP

Ask anonymously. Choose "private answer only" for personal matters (family, divorce, health). Moderators remove names and details before anything is published.

F4

Question number and statusMVP

Each question gets a number and a visible status: Received, Assigned, Being answered, In review, Answered, or Declined with a reason. Notifications by Telegram or email.

F5

Structured answer pageMVP

Short answer at the top (Qisqa javob), then evidence: Qur'an in Arabic with translation, hadith with source and grading, scholarly opinions, and the madhhab views where they differ. Related questions at the bottom.

F6

Named scholar and reviewerMVP

Every answer shows who wrote it and who approved it, with links to profiles listing education, ijazah and institution.

F7

Topic tree in two languagesMVP

Aqida · Taharat · Namoz · Ro'za · Zakot · Haj va Umra · Oila (nikoh, taloq) · Muomalot (savdo, bank, kripto) · Meros · Odob-axloq · Ayollar · Yangi musulmonlar · Zamonaviy masalalar. Each topic has a short intro and its most-read answers.

F8

Uzbek ↔ English translation flowMVP

Answers are written in the asker's language. A translator role produces the other version, and the original scholar or reviewer approves it. Each answer shows which versions exist.

F9

Telegram botMVP

Ask, get your question number, and receive the answer in Telegram. Also auto-posts newly published answers to a channel.

F10

Scholar workspaceMVP

Triage queue, my assignments, review queue, translation queue. The editor warns about duplicates and has buttons to insert an ayah or hadith with its reference.

F11

Methodology pageMVP

States the creed and madhhab the site follows, who the scholars are, how answers are reviewed, and what the site does not answer (for example, it doesn't judge named individuals or take sides in politics).

F12

Women's deskPhase 2

Questions routed to female scholars, with stricter privacy defaults.

F13

Tools: meros and zakot calculatorsPhase 2

Inheritance and zakat calculators following the Hanafi method, each linking to answers that explain the rules.

F14

AI "find my answer"Phase 2

Natural-language search that only quotes and links published, reviewed answers. If nothing matches, it offers to send the question to a scholar. It never writes rulings.

F15

Live sessions and audio answersPhase 2

Monthly live Q&A with a scholar, and short audio answers for people who prefer listening.

F16

Mobile app and offline savingPhase 2

Start as an installable web app; save answers to read offline. A native app only if usage justifies it.

Who uses it, and how a question moves

Public

Reader

  • Search and read all public answers
  • Switch Uzbek / English, Latin / Cyrillic
  • Share an answer link
Public

Asker

  • Ask with an account, Telegram, or anonymously
  • Track status by question number
  • Ask one follow-up
  • Save answers
Staff

Moderator

  • Screens new questions
  • Merges duplicates, links existing answers
  • Removes personal details
  • Routes by topic and language
Scholar

Scholar (ustoz)

  • Takes assigned questions
  • Writes answers with evidence
  • Can return a question to the moderator
Scholar

Senior reviewer

  • Approves or returns every answer
  • Final say on sensitive topics
  • Approves translations
Staff

Translator

  • Produces the second language
  • Keeps a shared glossary of terms
Staff

Admin

  • Adds scholars and checks credentials
  • Manages topics and policies
  • Sees reports

Question lifecycle

1 · AskerSubmittedGets number AQ-24817. Sees similar answers first.
2 · ModeratorTriageDuplicate? Off-topic? Personal details? Route to a topic and scholar.
3 · ScholarAnsweringShort answer, then evidence and references.
4 · ReviewerReviewApprove, or return with notes.
5 · SystemAnsweredAsker notified. Published if they agreed.
6 · TranslatorSecond languageTranslated, re-approved, then live in both languages.
Already answered: the moderator links the existing answer and closes the question within minutes.
Urgent (funeral, iddah, a time-bound act of worship): flagged and moved to the top of the queue.
Needs a personal meeting (divorce, inheritance dispute): declined publicly, with a referral to a local imam or the Fatvo hay'ati.

9 · Over to you

Decisions I need from you

Each has my recommendation. Reply with the number and your choice, or anything else you want to change.

D1
Madhhab and creed

Recommend: Hanafi fiqh, Maturidi creed as the default, as in Uzbekistan, showing other madhhabs' views where they differ.

D2
Relationship with the Muslim Board

Recommend: seek their endorsement or work with Board-approved scholars, and check registration rules with the Committee for Religious Affairs before launch. I can't give legal advice on this.

D3
Who are the first scholars?

Recommend: at least 3 scholars and 1 senior reviewer confirmed before building, including one woman for the women's desk later.

D4
Default Uzbek script

Recommend: Latin by default, with a one-tap Cyrillic switch, since many older readers still use Cyrillic.

D5
Must askers register?

Recommend: no. Allow Telegram or anonymous asking with a question number, and limit to 1 question per day to protect scholars' time.

D6
Use of AI

Recommend: no AI at launch. In Phase 2, add AI search that only quotes reviewed answers (F14).

D7
Name and domain

Recommend: keep "Aqeedah" as the working name for now. A name that also says "questions" (for example a savol-based name) may be easier for Uzbek users to find. Your call.

D8
Funding

Recommend: free for everyone, supported by donations and sponsors. No paid access to scholars.
